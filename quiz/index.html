Examination rules — read before you start

  1. The quiz opens in full screen. Leaving full screen, switching tabs, switching apps or minimising the window counts as a violation.
  2. You are allowed 3 violations. On the last one your quiz is submitted automatically with whatever you have answered.
  3. Every violation is timestamped and sent to your instructor immediately.
  4. The timer starts when you press Start Quiz and never pauses — not during a violation, not if you disconnect.
  5. Copy, paste, right-click, printing and developer shortcuts are disabled.
  6. If you refresh or lose connection briefly, reopen this page on the same browser to resume the same attempt with the remaining time. If that device will not come back, tell your instructor — they can let you resume on another one and grant you the time you lost.
  7. Your score is not shown here. Results are released by your instructor after the quiz.
  8. The Start button unlocks only after the reading time below has elapsed. That time is separate from your quiz duration.
